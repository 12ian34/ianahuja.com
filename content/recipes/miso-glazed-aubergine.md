## ingredients

- 3 medium/large aubergines
- 5 litres of water
- 200g salt
- 40g white miso
- 40ml mirin
- 10ml rice vinegar
- 10ml maple syrup
- 5ml toasted sesame oil
- 5ml sesame oil

## instructions

1. dissolve salt in water in large container or pan
2. cut aubergines into even, thick strips, each with some skin
3. add aubergines to salt water
4. rest paper towel on top
5. let brine for >4 hours
6. remove aubergine from brine
7. wash thoroughly
8. heat oven with grill on
9. add baking paper to baking tray
10. add aubergines, skin down to tray
11. brush them with a vegetable oil
12. place tray lower down (away from grill)
13. bake+grill for 20 min
14. add miso, mirin, rice vinegar, maple syrup to a small heated saucepan
15. whisk until miso dissolves
16. take off heat
17. add sesame oil
18. brush surfaces of aubergine with this mix
19. bake until glaze caramelises (few min)

serve with black and white sesame seeds, and maybe spring onion
