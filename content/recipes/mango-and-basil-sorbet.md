## ingredients

- 4 heaping cups frozen mango (~two 10-oz bags)
- 1/4 cup maple syrup
- 2 tbsp fresh lime juice

## instructions

1. food process mango to small shreds
2. scrape down sides
3. add maple syrup and lime juice
4. process more
5. make more creamy
6. freeze in sealed container for 2-3 hours
7. thaw 10 min before serving

2 weeks in freezer
