from mike simons

## ingredients

- 1 pound ripe red tomatoes
- 1/2 red bell pepper
- 1/2 yellow bell pepper
- 1/2 sweet onion
- 1/2 cucumber (english or salad)
- 2.5 spoons sherry vinegar
- 2.5 spoons red wine vinegar
- 1/2 tbsp salt
- 1 jalapeno
- 3/5 cups very good quality olive oil
- 1-2 cups water to thin out to desired density

## instructions

1. blend in a blender
2. finish with a hand-held blender

makes 4-5 servings

## potential additions

- garlic (1 clove)
- scallions
- basil leaves (garnish)
