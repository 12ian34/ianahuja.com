## ingredients

- 1/2 cup grated fresh coconut
- 1/2 cup curd
- 1/2 cup roasted channa or channa dal
- 1 onion
- 1 green chilli
- 3-4 garlic clove
- ginger
- salt

grind

## topping

- tsp mustard seeds
- 1-2 red chilli
- 3 curry leaves

## instructions

1. heat oil
2. add mustard seeds
3. add curry leaves
4. add chilli
5. pour into chutney
