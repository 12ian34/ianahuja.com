## instructions

bake fruit at 150 C

- rocha pear (1 hour)
- peach (45 min)
- plum (40 min)

## to serve

- greek yoghurt base
- toasted, semi-crushed mixed nuts (pistachio ideal)
- chia seeds (pre-soaked ideal)
- fresh mint
- fresh basil (optional)
- cocoa nibs (optional)
- maple syrup
