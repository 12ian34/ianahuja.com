## ingredients

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1/4 cup dried arbol chillies stemmed and seeded
- 450g tinned tomatoes
- 1 small tomatillo
- 2 tbsp white vinegar
- 1 clove garlic
- kosher salt

## instructions

1. heat oil on high
2. add chillies
3. cook but dont burn
4. orange = done
5. blend everything until smooth
6. pour blend into pot
7. bring to boil
8. simmer for 15
