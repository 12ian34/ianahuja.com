## ingredients

- 200g unsalted butter
- 120g plain flour
- 60g cocoa powder
- 25g whole milk powder
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp cayenne
- 340g 70% dark choc
- 200g granulated sugar
- 110g light brown sugar
- 4 large eggs
- 2 tsp vanilla extract

## instructions

1. preheat oven to 190
2. grease 13 inch by 9 inch metal pan with butter
3. add baking paper with overhang
4. grease baking paper
5. whisk flour, cocoa powder, milk powder, cinnamon, salt, cayenne
6. melt butter in saucepan
7. remove from heat
8. add choc, both sugars
9. mix until choc is glossy
10. add eggs one at a time
11. add vanilla extract
12. mix both
13. fold until no dry streaks
14. pour into pan
15. bake 25-30 mins until knife into centre comes out with a few moist crumbs attached
16. cool 15 min
17. put over wire rack and cool fully

## frosting

- 450g icing sugar
- 1 cup unsalted butter
- 1/2 cup cocoa powder
- 1/3 cup heavy cream
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt

1. mix sugar, butter, cocoa powder, cream, paprika, vanilla, salt
2. mix on medium high until sugar dissolved, 5 min
3. add frosting to brownie
4. sprinkle with paprika
