## ingredients

- 300g basmati rice
- 4 eggs
- 1 bag frozen peas
- 1 large red onion
- soy sauce
- sesame oil

## instructions

1. add peas to boiling water
2. blanch for 2 min
3. remove into ice bath
4. wait a bit
5. remove from bath and dry out well (takes a while)
6. add sesame oil to wok
7. heat
8. add eggs
9. beat a bit in pan
10. make like in between omelette and scrambled eggs
11. remove from wok
12. add sesame oil
13. add finely chopped red onion
14. cook until translucent
15. remove from wok
16. add rice to wok
17. break apart
18. get until almost toasting
19. add egg, peas and onions
20. add soy sauce
21. season

serve immediately
