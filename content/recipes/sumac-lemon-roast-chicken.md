for a spatchcock chicken (about 1.6-2kg bird, serves 4-6)

## ingredients

- 3 tbsp sumac
- 4 fat garlic cloves, crushed or microplaned
- zest of 1 lemon, plus juice of half
- 2 tsp fresh thyme leaves (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1 tbsp fresh oregano (or 1.5 tsp dried)
- 4 tbsp olive oil
- 1.5 tsp flaky sea salt
- plenty of black pepper
- pinch of chilli flakes (optional)

plus an extra teaspoon of sumac and a lemon wedge to finish at the table

## instructions

1. mix everything into a paste
2. loosen the skin over the breasts and thighs with your fingers and push half the rub directly onto the flesh underneath
3. rub the rest all over the outside
4. salt heavily an hour before roasting, ideally leave uncovered in the fridge overnight for crispier skin
5. roast at 220°C for around 40-45 minutes until the thickest part of the thigh hits 75°C
6. rest 10 minutes, finish with the extra sumac and a squeeze of lemon

scale up proportionally if your bird is bigger or you've got two
