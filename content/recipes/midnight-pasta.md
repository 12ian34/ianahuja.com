spaghetti aglio, olio e peperoncino

## ingredients

- spaghetti
- garlic
- olive oil
- dried chillies
- fresh parsley

## instructions

1. put all but parsley in cold pan
2. heat up
3. add parsley
4. cook a little
5. add water
6. finish spaghetti in the pan
7. add pasta water
8. should be a cream
9. keep stirring pasta to get starch
