## ingredients

- 700g strong white bread flour
- 300g wholemeal flour
- 700g water
- 150g starter
- 22g salt

## instructions

1. mix and autolyse for 45 min
2. add starter and salt
3. mix a bit
4. wait 30 mins
5. stretch and fold x10
6. wait 30 mins
7. stretch and fold x10
8. wait 30 mins
9. stretch and fold x10
10. wait 30 mins
11. stretch and fold x10

## prove

leave overnight in fridge (or for at least 5 hours)

- bubbles
- smell
- volume doubled

## shape

1. put flour on surface
2. use bench scraper
3. rough shape
4. sit on flour on board and cover
5. wait 30 mins
6. generously flour banneton
7. fold sides together interlocking
8. then front to back
9. fold in seams
10. add to banneton
11. flour again
12. final proof 1-3 hours

ready for oven is if you poke and it springs back a bit but not fully

## bake

1. preheat dutch oven at 250
2. when hot, add baking paper
3. add dough
4. score
5. bake with lid on for 20
6. remove lid
7. set oven to 230
8. put dutch oven on tray
9. go off colour not time
10. if ready, lid on
11. turn oven off and cure for 20 inside oven
