## dumplings

- 200g frozen spinach
- 15g fresh basil
- 10g fresh chives
- 2 cloves garlic
- lemon zest
- nutmeg
- 30g white miso
- 40g olive oil
- salt
- black pepper
- 60g fine quick cook polenta (<8 min)

## sauce

- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 20g unsalted butter
- 3 cloves garlic
- 280g sweet, ripe cherry toms
- fine salt
- chilli flakes
- 5 fresh sage leaves
- 5g fresh basil
- black pepper
- 3 tbsp water

## method

1. squeeze defrosted spinach to 100g
2. combine spinach, herbs, garlic, lemon, nutmeg, miso, oil, salt, pepper
3. blend to paste
4. heat milk until steaming
5. turn low
6. add polenta and whisk for 3 min until thick paste that comes off from sides/bottom
7. remove from heat and add green paste
8. stir and cool
9. oil hands
10. make 12 balls 30g each
11. transfer to container and cover to keep warm
12. saute all sauce ingredients
13. add dumplings to sauce and lid, and cook
14. serve with basil, olive oil, salt

serve quick, dumplings will harden
