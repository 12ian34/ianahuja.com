## ingredients

- 6 cups water
- 1/4 cup red kidney beans
- 1 black daal
- 1/4 chole ki daal
- 1.5 tsp salt
- 2 bay leaves
- 1.5 whole green chillis, finely chopped
- 4 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 1.5 medium white onions, chopped
- 7cm x 3cm x 3cm ginger root, finely chopped
- 3cm x 3cm x 3cm turmeric root, whole
- 1/2 tins of chopped tomatoes
- 1 heaped tsp cumin
- 1 heaped tsp coriander
- 1 heaped tbsp of very good quality garam masala (see below)
- 2.5 tbsp sunflower oil

## part 1

1. soak only kidney beans for 6+ hours
2. mix all beans/daal together
3. add water, salt, bay leaves, turmeric
4. pressure cook 30 min until beans are soft
5. remove turmeric root (could be reused)

## part 2

1. in large pan, add oil and heat
2. add onion, fry
3. add ginger, garlic, chilli, cook until golden brown
4. add cumin and coriander, stir fry until cumin changes colour
5. add chopped tomatoes, cook
6. add cooked mix from part 2 to beans/lentils from part 1
7. add garam masala

## garam masala

- 5 cloves
- 3 green cardamom pods
- 3 black cardamom (internal pips only)
- 8cm stick cinnamon
- 1 tbsp black peppercorn

grind all of the above

if it doesn't taste "garam" enough to you, just double everything ;)
