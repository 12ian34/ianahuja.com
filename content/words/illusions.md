---
title: "illusions"
description: "the twenties, the climb, and the false summit"
date: 2025-06-01
keywords: "poem, disillusionment, twenties, growth, false summit, philosophy"
---

the 20s - the age of disillusionment

but what is the illusion?

from the sheltered juvenile valley

to the rugged peaks of reality

while knowledge is a noble pursuit

which helps see more details minute

growth can breed arrogance that blinds

keep looking forward as you climb

beware the false summit

---

is this a rite of passage?

or can we instil better vision
